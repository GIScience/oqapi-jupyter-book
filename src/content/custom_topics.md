# Custom Topics

Besides the predefined topics, the Dashboard also lets you create your own topics or modify
existing ones. To do the former, select *Custom Topic* from the topic dropdown (see 1 in the
figure below). A new field then appears under the selected topic, where you can set a title for
your own topic. Below that, the field for the ohsome filter definition becomes editable. The
filter syntax is documented at [this link](https://docs.ohsome.org/ohsome-api/v1/filter.html) and
follows this pattern: *\<tag\>=\<value\> and geometry:\<point|line|polygon\>*. Alternatively, you can
choose any other topic and press the blue button next to the topic field (see 2 in the figure
below). This switches the topic to "Custom Topic" as described above. The ohsome filter of
the previously selected topic remains in the field, though, and can now be edited.
